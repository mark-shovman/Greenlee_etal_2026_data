**Supplementary Table S1. Per-participant results.** One row per participant (N = 58). Empty
cells: the participant did not take part in that task. *Training*: other recordings of the
participant were included in the limbus model's training set. *Age*: decade. *Refraction*:
refractive correction per eye, in dioptres; recorded for some participants, empty where not
recorded. *Kappa*: horizontal and vertical
kappa correction per eye, in degrees, median over the participant's grid blocks. *Fixations*:
fixation periods not rejected as outliers during kappa estimation, of all fixation periods, both
eyes pooled. *Accuracy* and *precision* (RMS-S2S), in degrees: the participant's median over
these fixations, weighted by the inverse number of fixations in the fixation's 3 × 3° cell, cells
with fewer than five fixations excluded; this is the participant's E50 in Figs 8 (all FOV) and 9
(central 15 × 15°). Brackets: 95% bootstrap confidence interval (2,000 resamples of the
participant's fixations, weights fixed). *Luminance episode-eyes*: trials × eyes without a saccade
or blink, of 6. *Constriction* and *gaze shift*: means over these, between the baseline window
(−500 to 0 ms) and the recovery window (1,000 to 1,500 ms). *Vergence runs*: visual search
repetitions completed. *Decoding*: leave-one-out depth decoding accuracy, within and across
participants (Fig. 15; chance 0.17). *Pairs discriminated / tested*: adjacent depth pairs whose
vergence distances differed at Bonferroni-adjusted p < 0.001, of the pairs with a valid reference
fixation for both targets. Full-precision values, fixation counts per weighting and unweighted
medians: `participants.csv`.
