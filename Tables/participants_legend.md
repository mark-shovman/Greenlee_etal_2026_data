**Supplementary Table S1. Per-participant results.** 
One row per participant (N = 58). 
Empty cells: the participant did not take part in that task. 
*Training*: other recordings of the participant were included in the limbus model's training set. 
*Age*: decade. 
*Refraction*: refractive correction per eye, in dioptres; recorded for some participants, empty where not recorded. 
*Kappa*: horizontal and vertical kappa correction per eye, in degrees, median over the participant's grid blocks (per-block values in Table S2). 
*Fixations*: valid fixation periods (at least 30% of the expected samples tracked, with head pose, and left after removing blinks), of all fixation periods, both eyes pooled. 
*Accuracy* and *precision* (RMS-S2S), in degrees: the participant's median over these fixations, weighted by the inverse number of fixations in the fixation's 3 × 3° cell, cells with fewer than five fixations excluded; this is the participant's E50 in Figs 8 (all FOV) and 9 (central 15 × 15°). Brackets: 95% bootstrap confidence interval (2,000 resamples of the participant's fixations, weights fixed). 
*Luminance episode-eyes*: trials × eyes without a saccade or blink, of 6. 
*Constriction* and *gaze shift*: means over these, between the baseline window (−500 to 0 ms) and the recovery window (1,000 to 1,500 ms). 
*Vergence runs*: visual search repetitions completed. 
*Decoding*: leave-one-out depth decoding accuracy, within and across participants (Fig. 15; chance 0.17). 
*Pairs discriminated / tested*: adjacent depth pairs whose vergence distances differed at Bonferroni-adjusted p < 0.001, of the pairs with a valid reference fixation for both targets.
